# Cinematic landing composition

Provenance: condensed and generalized from `/home/mike/projects/cloudfloo-v2/.agents/skills/cloudfloo-cinematic-landing` at commit `ce8517ed2e0fe375d45fe97319db859c6cebb405`.

## Narrative frame

- One viewport, one primary job. The hero is a controlled cinematic frame, not a dashboard.
- Keep the hero headline to eight words and at most three mobile lines.
- Use no more than two hero CTAs and one compact proof signal.
- Choose one persistent visual protagonist. Later sections reveal new meaning rather than swapping in unrelated decoration.
- Structure chapters as premise, proof, mechanism, trust, and conversion.
- Finish at an exact intentional frame; do not let the visual timeline stop between story beats.

## Film and contrast

Use a poster-first layer and explicit object-cover geometry. Copy may receive a bounded local gradient or light translucent underlay when variable footage reduces contrast. Tune it on bright and dark frames. The treatment must keep the film present; do not cover the entire section with an opaque slab.

Rows, labels, and calls to action exist in their stable base state. Intersection motion may transform them only after enhancement initializes. A missed observer callback, reverse scroll, refresh, or exception must never leave content invisible.

## Motion and chapters

Reveal hierarchy with short, capped entry staggers. Keep persistent elements stable across chapter transitions. Scroll-linked transformations must be reversible and tied to narrative progress. Avoid scroll-jacking, excessive pinned distance, or simultaneous competing focal points.

## Mobile and fallback

Recompose rather than shrink. Shorten line length, simplify layer count, preserve focal crops, keep the CTA in the first viewport when required, and prevent horizontal overflow. Reduced motion, save-data, and decoder failure use a strong static poster with all content visible.

## Acceptance

Test 360×800, 390×844, 768×1024, and 1440×900. Scroll forward/backward, refresh mid-page, resize during a chapter, fail media, enable reduced motion/save-data, traverse by keyboard, and verify the last frame. Reject crowded heroes, unrelated card grids, hidden base content, weak contrast, and motion without narrative purpose.
