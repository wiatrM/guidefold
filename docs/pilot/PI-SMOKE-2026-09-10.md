## Interpretation and limit

This is evidence that the Pi → bridge → Go service → hidden verifier pipeline is executable and
that `ASK` is observable without leaking a body. It says nothing about whether retrieval selected
the right skill, whether a skill helped, or whether proof-gated delivery improves developer task
success. The next valid run needs a frozen multi-task bank, pinned model/harness versions, paired
baseline conditions, and either deterministic acceptance checks or two blind human judgements.
