## Interpretation

We have a substantive positive result for LLM-authored search maps: pooled SRA Recall@10 rose from 56.69% to 65.22%, Complete@4 from 42.31% to 47.19%. The gain over the same-budget source-metadata control (+7.45/+4.19 pp) is more informative for the LLM contribution than the flat comparison alone.

The product hypothesis is that generated descriptions of task families can recover skills a flat search misses. The result supports an experimental search path. It does not establish that moving through more hierarchy levels helps, that generated parent instructions preserve meaning, or that agents complete more real monorepo tasks.

The subsequent [retained-output mechanism audit](2026-09-10-r22-mechanism-audit.md) independently reproduced all 21,600 text-arm scope selections. Of 551 Complete@4 gains against flat, 200 required a skill outside the original top-50; 288 queries lost completeness. Pooled intervals remained positive when repeated gold sets were grouped. The audit also makes the main generalization risk explicit: 636 labelled skills, 25,626 web distractors, and source-prefix groups aligned with query families. These findings are post hoc, with no new experiment.

Both CHAMP and TheoremQA regress against flat; dataset-bootstrap intervals include zero. The corpus-derived prefix hierarchy is not a natural monorepo. The public release and map-only diagnostic sequence make the analysis exploratory. RRF and the first-result anchor are explicit parts of the method.

Pi r3 is a separate, narrower engineering result: 3 SEARCH, 2 USE, 2 ASK, zero delivered bodies and zero reported used skills. Its selected skills overlap the gold set 0/2. The benchmark prompt prescribed SEARCH/USE/ASK, so it cannot prove spontaneous adoption of the bootstrap skill or correct task execution.

Use: “In one Pi replay, missing proof produced ASK and no skill body was treated as loaded.” Preserve sample size and the selection failure in the details.

Do not claim a scientific breakthrough, better user productivity, universal accuracy, automatic safe skill promotion, default deployment of R22, or a guarantee against wrong instructions. Publication potential has improved, but novelty and end-to-end benefit remain open; a numeric paper-acceptance probability is not measured.
